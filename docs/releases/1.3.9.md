# [TaCZ] Weapon Research & Blueprints 1.3.9

The Minecraft 1.21.1 NeoForge version is now a stable release. This update
uses the latest Fzzy Config hotfix while keeping the progression features
introduced in the NeoForge beta.

## Highlights

- Explore for blueprints, learn recipes, and craft at tiered Workbenches in
  singleplayer or multiplayer.
- Research weapons, ammo, and attachments through the Research Bench and
  Tech Tree. Use the Blueprint Analyzer, Blueprint Journal, fragments, and
  Research Data to develop your arsenal.
- Trade with the Gunsmith and Ballistics Researcher, or customize progression
  through Research Designer and datapacks.
- Built and checked with **Fzzy Config 0.7.7+fix1+1.21+neoforge**, including
  its updated rendering utility.

## Updating

Back up your world, then replace the NeoForge beta JAR on the server and every
client with this release. Existing NeoForge worlds and progress can be kept;
no configuration reset is required. Update Fzzy Config to its Minecraft
1.21.1 NeoForge hotfix build as well.

For a first installation, use a new Minecraft 1.21.1 NeoForge world. Migration
of Forge 1.20.1 worlds and player progress is not supported. **TaCZ Magazines
integration remains Forge-only.** Custom datapacks must target Minecraft 1.21.1.

## Requirements

- Minecraft **1.21.1**, **Java 21**, and **NeoForge 21.1.233 or newer in the
  21.1 series**.
- The **unofficial NeoForge TaCZ port 1.1.8 or newer**; tested with
  **1.1.8-hotfix-r7**.
- **Fzzy Config 0.7.7 or later in the 0.7 series for NeoForge**; tested with
  **0.7.7+fix1+1.21+neoforge** and **Kotlin for Forge's NeoForge variant 5.12.0**.
- Optional: **JEI 19.x** or **EMI 1.1.x** for Minecraft 1.21.1 NeoForge;
  tested with **JEI 19.21.0.247** and **EMI 1.1.24+1.21.1**.
