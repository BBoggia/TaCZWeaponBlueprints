# [TaCZ] Weapon Research & Blueprints 1.3.1-beta.1

This beta brings physical Scrap, tiered weapon analysis, and a redesigned Tech
Tree to the 1.3 series. Research Data can now produce weapon fragments and
blueprints, with configurable rewards and target pools.

## Highlights

- **Physical research currency** — Carry Scrap to pay for research and Analyzer
  actions. Banked Research Points (RP) remains a configurable option.
- **Three Blueprint Analyzer tiers** — Weapon and Analyzer tiers determine the
  chances of a blueprint, fragments, or no reward. By default, only attempts
  above the Analyzer's tier can produce nothing. Datapacks can change every tier
  pair, cost, fragment amount, and reconstruction requirement.
- **Research Data analysis** — Notes target Tier 1 weapons, Reports target Tiers
  1–2, and Dossiers target Tiers 1–3 by default. Save matching analysis fragments
  to reconstruct a blueprint.
- **Clearer research screens** — The Tech Tree separates branches, keeps weapon
  artwork visible farther out, and expands when nothing is selected. The
  Blueprint Analyzer has a new layout with clearer results and fewer repeated
  messages.
- **Updated workstations** — New models distinguish the Research Bench,
  crafting Workbench, and Blueprint Analyzer tiers. Each tier has a direct
  crafting recipe.
- **Simpler settings** — Settings are grouped by purpose, percentages and hold
  times use familiar units, and choices have clearer names and explanations.

## Fixes

- Loaded weapons can be analyzed or recycled: successful actions return their
  ammo to your inventory and drop any overflow at your feet.
- Multiplayer settings display the server's values when you join.
- Improved Analyzer previews and recovery after failed actions, retained
  research progress across respawns, and corrected Tech Tree navigation and
  refresh behavior.

## Updating

Install the same beta on the server and every client. Back up your world and
configuration before testing this beta on an existing save.

Scrap and Research Data analysis are the defaults when the new economy settings
are absent. Existing saved RP is preserved and is not automatically converted
into Scrap. Explicit currency and Data settings remain in effect. Review the
[currency migration guide](../research-currency-migration.md) to retain the old
economy or convert saved RP.

Existing datapacks can still override the bundled rules. Blueprint Analyzers
now occupy two blocks side by side; allow room for both halves. Upgrade kits
have been removed in favor of direct station recipes.

## Requirements

- Minecraft 1.20.1 with Forge 47.x; built with Forge 47.3.0.
- Timeless and Classics Zero (TaCZ) 1.1.8 or later in the 1.1 series; development
  testing uses 1.1.8-hotfix.
- Fzzy Config 0.7.6 or later in the 0.7 series, with its required dependencies.
- Java 17. JEI and EMI are optional.
