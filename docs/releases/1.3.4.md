# [TaCZ] Weapon Research & Blueprints 1.3.4

Version 1.3.4 fixes learned recipes disappearing from multiplayer Workbenches
and adds optional support for TaCZ Magazines, including configurable magazine
access and protection for magazines inserted into guns at the Blueprint Analyzer.

## Highlights

- **Multiplayer crafting restored:** Learned recipes appear at all three
  crafting Workbench levels on dedicated servers, with their normal progression
  requirements. TaCZ's ammo categories are available alongside guns and attachments.
- **Optional magazine crafting:** With TaCZ Magazines **0.2.0-Beta** installed,
  craft empty magazines at any of the three Workbench levels or TaCZ's original
  ammo Workbench. The add-on's recipe costs and pack overrides apply.
- **Choose how magazines unlock:** Material-only crafting is the default.
  Servers can instead require a learned compatible weapon. Learning any weapon
  in a shared family grants access to that family's magazines. Basic magazines
  can remain available for found or traded guns.
- **Separate magazine tier settings:** Choose minimum Workbench levels for basic
  and extended magazines. Both start at Tier 1. Loading and fitting magazines
  still follows the add-on's rules.
- **Keep inserted magazines safe:** The Blueprint Analyzer asks you to eject
  an inserted magazine, even an empty one, before processing its gun. After
  ejection, normal weapon analysis and ammo recovery apply.

## Fixes

- Magazine recipe changes refresh open Workbenches after reload. Players already
  online also receive the required recipes when magazine discovery recovers
  during another player's login.
- Empty magazine lists give guidance about weapon learning, Workbench levels,
  and filters when blueprint progression is enabled.

## Updating

Install **1.3.4 on both the server and every client**, with one version of this
mod in each `mods` folder. This release updates the network protocol to 66;
older clients and servers cannot connect to it.

Back up your world before updating. Existing learned blueprints and saved
progress are retained. TaCZ Magazines is optional; the integration supports
**0.2.0-Beta** specifically and does not require installing it for ordinary
weapon progression.

## Requirements

- Minecraft **1.20.1**, Forge **47.x**, and Java **17**.
- **Timeless and Classics Zero (TaCZ) 1.1.8 or later in the 1.1 series**;
  development testing uses 1.1.8-hotfix.
- **Fzzy Config 0.7.6 or later in the 0.7 series**, plus its required dependencies,
  including Kotlin for Forge.
