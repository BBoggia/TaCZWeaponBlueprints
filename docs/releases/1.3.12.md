# [TaCZ] Weapon Research & Blueprints 1.3.12

This update fixes a dedicated-server crash that could occur when another mod loaded the Creative tabs. It also removes Classic view from the Research Bench on both loaders.

## Highlights

- The Research Bench now opens the lane-based Tech Tree for all players. If you used Classic, your saved lane grouping and confirmation settings carry over automatically. If a price or route changes while you hold **Research**, release and hold again to confirm the updated purchase.
- Switching between **Weapons**, **Attachments**, and **Ammo** closes the previous blueprint’s details and Research button. Switching between branch and weapon-type lanes keeps you in the same tab.
- On small screens, the toolbar uses shorter labels so each tab and button has its own click area.
- The in-game mod summary and CurseForge description now explain how to unlock weapon recipes and where to craft them.

### Forge 1.20.1

- Fzzy Config groups the old camera, grid, branch label, and minimap options under **Legacy Layout**.
- Verified magazine recipe discovery after a datapack reload on a dedicated server with TaCZ 1.2.0-hotfix and TaCZ Magazines 0.2.0-Beta.

### NeoForge 1.21.1

- Personal Settings now shows lane grouping, purchase confirmation and its hold duration, and reward notifications. **Restore Tree Appearance** resets lanes to **Branches**.
- Fixed a case where a delayed server reply could appear against a newer selection after the Tech Tree screen reopened.

## Updating

Install the matching addon build on the server and every client. Existing players can open the Research Bench with their current configuration.
