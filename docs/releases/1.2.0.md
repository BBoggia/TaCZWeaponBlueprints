# [TaCZ] Weapon Research & Blueprints 1.2.0

Version 1.2 makes large Tech Trees easier to navigate and turns long-term
research goals into clear, manageable plans. You can pin a target, follow its
complete route, check your material progress, and quickly find research you
can afford now.

## Highlights

- **Pinned research goals** — Pin a weapon from the Research Bench to keep its
  route and progress available while you explore the tree. A direct return
  action brings the tracked target back into view.
- **Complete route guidance** — A pinned target highlights the exact route
  confirmed by the server, including every prerequisite needed for the
  purchase. RP and material progress show what you have and what remains.
- **Affordable Now filter** — Narrow the Tech Tree to unlocks that your current
  RP and inventory can cover. The check includes the complete research route,
  not only the selected weapon's individual price.
- **Interactive minimap** — Use the fullscreen minimap to understand the shape
  of a large tree, see the current viewport, and move quickly by clicking or
  dragging. Personal Settings can show it automatically, keep it visible, or
  hide it.
- **Recent unlock history** — The Blueprint Journal records recent blueprint
  unlocks so you can review the progress made during your current play session
  and across reconnects.

## Fixes

- Prevented rejected or expired route guidance from remaining highlighted as
  though it were still confirmed by the server.
- Kept Affordable Now checks responsive when a shared server is processing
  several research requests.
- Prevented the minimap from covering persistent Tech Tree controls on compact
  screens.

## Updating

- No world migration is required. Existing learned blueprints, discoveries,
  Research Points, research rules, and supported datapacks remain compatible.
- Clients and servers must update to 1.2.0 together because the synchronized
  research guidance uses an updated network protocol.
- Remove older [TaCZ] Weapon Research & Blueprints JARs from the `mods` folder
  before installing 1.2.0. Keep only one version installed.
- Fzzy Config 0.7.6 or newer in the supported 0.7.x line is required on both
  client and server.

## Requirements

- Minecraft 1.20.1
- Forge 47.x; validated with Forge 47.3.0
- Timeless and Classics Zero 1.1.8-hotfix (`1.1.8` up to, but not including,
  `1.2`)
- Fzzy Config 0.7.6 up to, but not including, 0.8
- Kotlin for Forge 4.11.x

JEI 15.x and EMI 1.1.x remain optional client-side integrations.
