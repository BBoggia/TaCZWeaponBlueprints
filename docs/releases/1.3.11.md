# [TaCZ] Weapon Research & Blueprints 1.3.11

The Research Bench has a new Tech Tree on both loaders. Find the weapon you want, follow its prerequisites, and see what you can afford at your current bench. Both builds support the official Timeless and Classics Zero (TaCZ) 1.2.0-hotfix.

## Highlights

### Both builds

- Browse the tree by branch or weapon type. **Ctrl+F** searches, **F** shows the whole tree, and **N** jumps to a ready blueprint or a suggestion. Choose **Classic view** for the original canvas and minimap. The bench remembers your preference.
- Amber tiles mark research the server confirms you can buy. You can select or research a blueprint while those checks run. Select a weapon to inspect its currency cost, material pages, bench tier, and alternative prerequisites. A blueprint keeps its own bench tier when an earlier prerequisite needs a higher one.
- Holding **Research** or Enter confirms the displayed purchase. If the target or price changes, learning and printing both require a fresh confirmation. Printing clears the completed selection, and delayed replies preserve a newer selection.

### NeoForge 1.21.1

- **Quick Setup → Pace** offers Relaxed, Normal, and Hard. Each changes blueprint loot, currency drops, and research prices together. **Advanced Settings** lets operators tune those values individually.
- On new servers with bench tiers enabled, Tier 2 research costs double the base currency price and Tier 3 costs triple. Existing configurations start the new tier multipliers at 100%. Material costs keep their datapack values.
- Automatic tiers account for a weapon’s depth in the tree and its strength. In the bundled tree the M249 is Tier 2, and the M4A1, HK416D, and M107 are Tier 3. Players keep their learned blueprints when a weapon changes tier.
- Gunsmiths draw from revised weapon pools. Previously stocked offers can disappear if their target changes tier. Ballistics Researchers sell Notes for 2 Scrap, Reports for 5, and Dossiers for 12. Their targeted fragment bundles are cheaper too.
- The Blueprint Journal and Blueprint Analyzer have clearer controls and status colors. Personal Settings includes Tech Tree view and lane preferences.

## Updating

Choose the jar for your Minecraft version and install the same addon build on the server and every client. Both loaders accept the original TaCZ 1.2.0 and the 1.2.0-hotfix.
