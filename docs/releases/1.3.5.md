# [TaCZ] Weapon Research & Blueprints 1.3.5

Version 1.3.5 fixes a Research Bench crash when you have the required materials
but not enough Scrap. Selecting that research now shows the missing currency
and keeps the Research Bench usable.

## Highlights

- Fixed the selection crash for research with learned prerequisites and for
  Scrap-only research costs.
- When a pack uses the same item as currency and as a research material, the
  preview counts the currency payment first and shows the materials still needed.
- Research that produces a physical blueprint now shows the configured currency
  balance correctly.
- If a research preview cannot be prepared, research stays disabled until a
  valid preview is available instead of crashing the server.

## Updating

Replace the previous JAR with **1.3.5 on the server and every client**, keeping
one version of this mod in each `mods` folder. Existing learned blueprints,
Research Points, and settings are retained. No currency conversion or
configuration reset is needed when updating from 1.3.4.

## Requirements

- Minecraft **1.20.1**, Forge **47.x**, and Java **17**.
- **Timeless and Classics Zero (TaCZ) 1.1.8 or later in the 1.1 series**.
- **Fzzy Config 0.7.6 or later in the 0.7 series**, plus its required dependencies,
  including Kotlin for Forge.
- TaCZ Magazines remains optional; its integration supports **0.2.0-Beta**.
