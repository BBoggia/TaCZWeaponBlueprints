# Development design documents

`../blueprint-knowledge-flow-phase-8.md` closes the sequence with reversible
discovery-pacing presets, a catalog-aware permission-gated setup assistant,
preview plus literal confirmation, player-neutral export evidence, and
packaged release contracts without changing protocol or player saves.

`../blueprint-knowledge-flow-phase-7.md` adds first-hour Journal onboarding,
server-filtered earning help, and optional JEI/EMI generic information while
preserving hidden-catalog and recipe-transfer authority boundaries.

`../blueprint-knowledge-flow-phase-6.md` completes synchronized progression
exemptions and additive starting grants with live access, idempotent knowledge,
reload/config publication, diagnostics, and migration-safe behavior.

`../blueprint-knowledge-flow-phase-5.md` activates the two-slot Blueprint
Analyzer. Physical TaCZ inputs now receive a server-authored reverse-engineering
preview and explicit rollback-safe action, while duplicate recycling and
Research Data redemption remain contextual uses of the same input. The exact
protocol advances once to 26 and no registry or player-save migration is
introduced.

`../blueprint-knowledge-flow-phase-4.md` adds strict selector-resolved
reverse-engineering policy, canonical physical TaCZ item resolution, a pure
non-mutating eligibility evaluator, bounded ammo batch derivation, additive
blueprint provenance, and reload diagnostics. The live Analyzer action, second
slot, packets, and protocol remain deferred to Phase 5.

`../blueprint-knowledge-flow-phase-3.md` activates direct Research Tree learning
with complete inventory/RP rollback and keeps the compatibility-only physical
tree-output mode without changing the Analyzer or protocol.

`../blueprint-knowledge-flow-phase-2.md` adds one preflightable atomic
capability mutation and the shared policy-aware learning service, routes
physical blueprint use and legacy migration through it, and preserves the
Research Tree's physical output plus protocol/save/workstation compatibility.

`../blueprint-knowledge-flow-phase-1.md` adds the closed learning-origin and
result-mode vocabulary plus a pure typed access-decision policy. It freezes
ordinary and migration precedence, prerequisite-bypass and award eligibility
semantics, and keeps every live transaction on its characterized protocol-25
Phase 0 path.

`../blueprint-knowledge-flow-phase-0.md` begins the direct-learning and physical
reverse-engineering follow-up. It freezes the three acquisition routes, shared
learning authority, two-slot Blueprint Analyzer destination, policy vocabulary,
atomic transaction and RP-award rules, safe equipment handling, compatibility,
and current protocol-25/one-slot characterization without changing live
gameplay.

`../research-workstations-phase-8.md` closes the workstation redesign with
whole-runtime packaged-JAR requirements, final model-content validation,
candidate-report metadata, current protocol-25 evidence, JDK-17 verification,
and an explicit boundary around the hands-on checks automation cannot claim.

`../research-workstations-phase-7.md` replaces the Recycler's temporary iron
cube with final project-owned art, a resource-pack-native shaped block model,
four-way presentation, matching outline/collision geometry, and packaged-asset
regression gates without changing gameplay authority or compatibility.

`../research-workstations-phase-6.md` makes the dedicated Blueprint Recycler
survival-craftable, unlocks its recipe from contextual blueprint/Bench/Research
Data inventory discovery, exposes it beside the Research Bench in Functional
Blocks, and preserves protocol/save compatibility ahead of the final art pass.

`../research-workstations-phase-5.md` removes the unreachable combined-menu
adapter. The Research Bench is now slotless and research-only at the menu,
action, preview, packet, and screen layers; the Blueprint Recycler exclusively
owns duplicate and Research Data turn-ins. The narrowed wire contract advances
matching clients and servers to protocol 25.

`../research-workstations-phase-4.md` completes the player-facing Research
Bench cutover: the existing Bench now opens directly into one permanent,
edge-to-edge, Research-only tree while the old combined server surface remains
temporarily intact as an unreachable compatibility adapter.

`../research-workstations-phase-3.md` activates the dedicated Blueprint
Recycler and documents its conventional screen, correlated request lifecycle,
input-bound feedback, accessible full-detail presentation, and runtime QA
boundary.

`../research-workstations-phase-2.md` registers the additive Blueprint Recycler
and implements its one-slot server menu, slot-scoped atomic Research Data
transactions, stale input protection, quick-move, preview synchronization, and
close-return lifecycle while keeping its screen and activation deferred.

`../research-workstations-phase-1.md` records the earlier separation of the
research-only and then-future single-slot Blueprint Recycler action/preview
contracts. It appended the bounded packet IDs and advanced the exact network
protocol to 24 before Phase 2 and Phase 3 activated them.

`../research-workstations-phase-0.md` is the historical baseline for separating
the Research Bench's permanent fullscreen Research Tree from the additive
Blueprint Recycler workstation. It froze player journeys, ownership,
registry/network compatibility, transaction safety, state coverage, and
release verification before the later phases completed that split.

`research-tree-fullscreen-redesign.md` is the canonical plan for the current
Research Bench fullscreen redesign.

The current unified-overview follow-up is recorded in
`research-tree-unified-overview-phase-0.md` through
`research-tree-unified-overview-phase-8.md`. Its Phase 8 record distinguishes
automated/startup evidence from the still-required hands-on publication matrix.

New fullscreen work should link to the canonical document and record any
intentional contract change there before implementation.

The checked-in wrapper remains pinned to Gradle 8.8 for the Minecraft 1.20.1
ForgeGradle/MixinGradle toolchain. `--warning-mode all` currently attributes the
Gradle 9 warning to those plugins' use of `org.gradle.util.VersionNumber`, not
to this project's build logic. Do not advance the wrapper to Gradle 9 until the
toolchain versions used by this branch support it.

The in-game follow-up begins with
`../research-tech-tree-unified-phase-0.md`. It freezes the corrective product
contract: Branches and All Weapons stay weapon-only, Tech Tree owns three
single-canvas domain graphs, type/lane classifications become authoring hints
rather than visible partitions, tiers cannot regress along prerequisites, and
the current 53/0/0 live publication is measured explicitly against the complete
53/95/24 default target before later phases expand research authority and
replace the lane-based layout.
`../research-tech-tree-unified-phase-1.md` generalizes the synchronized
server-authoritative graph to every research-enabled blueprint kind while
deriving every legacy presentation, skeleton, layout, and projection from an
explicit weapon-only induced subgraph. It also permits partial legacy member
tables on protocol 19 and routes global non-weapon search results only to valid
typed Tech Tree placements, without enabling the bundled attachment or
ammunition research rules yet.
`../research-tech-tree-unified-phase-2.md` enables all 95 pinned attachment and
24 pinned ammunition recipes with exact tier-scaled policies, adds conservative
kind-specific add-on fallbacks, preserves the 53-weapon legacy projections, and
freezes the intentionally independent Phase 2 nodes before Phase 3 authors the
final prerequisite topology.
`../research-tech-tree-unified-phase-3.md` replaces that staging state with
three connected, single-entry, tier-monotonic domain trees; preserves the
weapon-only legacy graph; keeps default domains independently researchable;
and adds reusable publication topology evidence plus packaged reachability
gates.
`../research-tech-tree-unified-phase-4.md` derives one immutable relationship
index from the synchronized public graph, publishes every genuine cross-domain
edge once with reciprocal requirement/unlock navigation, and makes portal
activation verify its exact destination against the current publication.
`../research-tech-tree-unified-phase-5.md` routes all three domain layouts
through the shared layered kernel, retains six bottom-to-top authored tier
bands and same-tier steps, removes lane and category geometry, and atomically
reflows Tech Tree canvases with the same bounded client layout policy.
