# [TaCZ] Weapon Research & Blueprints 1.3.0

Version 1.3 expands weapon progression beyond the Tech Tree with separate
tiered Research Benches and crafting Workbenches, plus target-specific
Blueprint Fragments. Servers and modpacks can also place advancement,
workstation, or custom milestone requirements at meaningful points in the
progression.

## Highlights

- **Three Research Bench tiers** — Progress from the Research Bench to Advanced
  and Experimental versions, with direct recipes for every tier.
- **Dedicated crafting Workbenches** — Three Workbench levels open TaCZ's
  familiar weapon-crafting screen. The server shows and crafts only learned or
  exempt recipes available at the current Workbench level. Direct recipes let
  you build any Workbench level without starting from Level 1.
- **Blueprint Fragments** — Blueprint loot can produce target-specific
  fragments without increasing the total research-item supply. Archive them in
  the Blueprint Analyzer to build progress toward the matching weapon.
- **Configurable fragment results** — A completed fragment set can discount
  matching Tech Tree research, reconstruct a protected blueprint, or return
  configured RP for an already learned target.
- **Progression Gates** — Pack authors can require vanilla advancements,
  Research Bench tiers, or custom server milestones for selected research and
  crafting. Hidden requirements remain disclosure-safe in player interfaces.
- **Content-pack workstation support** — Servers can assign crafting tiers to
  compatible third-party TaCZ workstations and choose a controlled fallback
  for unknown workstations.
- **Approachable crafting controls** — Servers can choose a simple ammo or
  attachment strategy, then optionally use advanced category, subgroup, and
  exact blueprint overrides without changing Tech Tree participation or
  blueprint-free knowledge rules.
- **Expanded administration and authoring** — Research inspection, status,
  catalog exports, and progression commands now cover workstation tiers,
  Blueprint Fragments, and custom Progression Gate criteria.

## Fixes

- Kept crafting Workbench tabs initialized while recipes remain unavailable
  until the initial server crafting-access response arrives.
- Republished tier and gate policy after live settings changes and repaired a
  missed integrated-server configuration update.
- Prevented progression exemptions from bypassing configured workstation tiers
  or Progression Gates.
- Kept large crafting-access responses within the network payload limit and
  prevented stale responses from applying to a newly opened workstation. An
  incomplete response receives one bounded retry.
- Prevented completed fragment sets from becoming unreachable when an exact
  threshold is higher than the normal retention cap.
- Fixed dedicated-server startup when common configuration initializes without
  client-only settings controls.

## Updating

- Existing player data migrates automatically to version 4. Learned
  blueprints, discoveries, Research Points, recent unlocks, and supported
  datapacks remain intact.
- Existing version-2 server settings migrate to Classic progression with
  Blueprint Fragments disabled. Operators can opt into tiered progression and
  fragments after reviewing the new settings.
- Existing version-3 server settings retain research-profile crafting behavior;
  the new crafting strategies and overrides begin at neutral defaults.
- Clients and servers must update together because 1.3.0 uses network protocol
  55.
- The ordinary crafting recipe for TaCZ's legacy Gun Smith Table is disabled
  by default. Existing tables, items, assets, and Creative inventory access
  remain available, and a higher-priority datapack can restore the recipe.
- Research profile format 3 adds workstation tiers, Blueprint Fragment rules,
  and Progression Gates. Format 4 adds independently resolved crafting policy.
  Existing supported profiles remain compatible, while new research catalog
  exports use format 20.
- Full Blueprint Journal entries and selected Tech Tree details show the
  disclosed crafting Workbench requirement. Operator inspection and format-20
  exports report the independent research and crafting assignments.
- Remove older [TaCZ] Weapon Research & Blueprints JARs before installing
  1.3.0. Keep only one version in the `mods` folder.

## Requirements

- Minecraft 1.20.1
- Forge 47.x; validated with Forge 47.3.0
- Timeless and Classics Zero 1.1.8-hotfix (`1.1.8` up to, but not including,
  `1.2`)
- Fzzy Config 0.7.6 up to, but not including, 0.8
- Kotlin for Forge 4.11.x

JEI 15.x and EMI 1.1.x remain optional client-side integrations.
